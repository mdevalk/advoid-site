Ads still showing up somewhere?

No filter list catches everything. If you find a site where ads are getting through, email the URL and we'll take a look at adding a rule for it.

How do I turn Advoid off for one site?

Open the Advoid icon in your toolbar and toggle "This site." That disables protection only for the site you're currently on — everywhere else stays blocked.

Does Advoid collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy for the full details.
